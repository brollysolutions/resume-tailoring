# Engineering Manager

## Role Overview
We are looking for an Engineering Manager to lead our Core Platform team. You will be responsible for people management, project delivery, and technical strategy.

## Requirements
- 10+ years of total experience, with 3+ years in a leadership role.
- Experience managing cross-functional teams of 5-10 engineers.
- Strong ability to translate business requirements into technical roadmaps.
- Excellent communication and mentorship skills.
- Master's degree in Business Administration or Engineering Management is a plus.
- Proven track record of shipping complex software products at scale.
